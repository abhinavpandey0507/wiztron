Wiztron

A workshop for builders, disguised as a club.

Motto

We build together. Every circuit soldered, every line of code shipped, every idea prototyped — it happens shoulder to shoulder, never alone.

Vision

To turn the campus into a launchpad — a place where curious students become engineers who ship real hardware, real software, and real impact.

Culture

Open workbenches, open source, open doors. From first-year beginners to final-year builders, everyone gets a seat at the table and a soldering iron.
